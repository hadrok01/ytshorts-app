Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

What ytshorts is

ytshorts is a personal desktop tool. Only its developer uses it, on their own computer, to manage their own YouTube channels.

What data it accesses

After the developer signs in with Google, ytshorts uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos to the developer's own channels and set their titles, descriptions and playlists;
  • post and read comments on those videos;
  • read view, retention and subscriber statistics of those channels.

It does not access any other person's account or data.

How data is stored and shared

Sign-in tokens and statistics are stored only on the developer's computer. They are not sent to any other server, sold, or shared with third parties.

YouTube and Google

ytshorts uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions.

Contact

Questions can be opened as an issue on this project's GitHub repository.
